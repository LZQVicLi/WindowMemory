# Share channel icons

X, Facebook, WhatsApp, Telegram, Reddit and WeChat SVGs are from [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/30053a0f492fd192740ad001031b3091be060986/icons), commit `30053a0f492fd192740ad001031b3091be060986`.

LinkedIn is from [Simple Icons 11.15.0](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/11.15.0/icons/linkedin.svg).

The SVG artwork is provided under CC0-1.0; see LICENSE-CC0.md. Brand names and logos remain trademarks of their respective owners. These icons identify sharing destinations and do not imply endorsement.

The checked-in transparent PNGs in `Resources/ShareIcons/` are 96 × 96 renders of these sources, tinted in the app. No icon CDN or runtime network dependency is used. Native share, clipboard and mail buttons use macOS SF Symbols.
